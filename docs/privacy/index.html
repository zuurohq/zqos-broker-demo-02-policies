ZQOS · Broker Demo 02

Privacy Policy

Effective 25 August 2026

This policy applies only to the private, non-production ZQOS Broker Demo 02 Tradovate OAuth activation. It does not apply to a production service, live trading, funded accounts, public application access, or order execution.

Data accessed

The activation is limited to two read-only Tradovate Demo requests:

  1. GET /v1/auth/me, to verify the authenticated user identity.
  2. GET /v1/account/list, to identify one intended active Demo account.

It does not request or access orders, positions, contracts, market data, alerts, chat, account-risk settings, or live-environment resources.

Credentials and storage

The OAuth client secret and access token are handled through protected local input and in-memory buffers. They are not committed to source control, written to evidence files, included in prompts, or logged. The activation credential is revoked and removed immediately after the permitted evidence is secured.

Sanitized evidence may retain non-secret identifiers and cryptographic hashes needed to prove the Demo-only origin, request order, selected active Demo account, credential revocation, and absence of prohibited effects. Raw provider responses are not published.

Sharing and use

No accessed data is sold, advertised against, redistributed, or used for live trading. Sanitized evidence is retained privately for project governance, security review, and auditability.

Security boundary

The client rejects the Tradovate live origin, arbitrary paths, non-GET provider requests after token exchange, ambiguous account selection, and permission settings outside its reviewed least-privilege envelope. Successful authentication does not authorize trading or production use.

Questions or removal requests

Questions about this private project or requests to remove retained sanitized evidence may be raised through the project owner. Credential revocation and cleanup take priority over evidence preservation.
