Terms of Use

Effective 25 August 2026

These terms govern the private, non-production ZQOS Broker Demo 02 Tradovate OAuth activation.

Permitted purpose

The activation may be used solely to verify OAuth, authenticated identity, and selection of one intended active Tradovate Demo account. It is a bounded technical validation and not a trading service, investment product, or public application.

Prohibited use

The activation must not:

  • access the Tradovate live API environment;
  • access funded or prop-firm accounts;
  • submit, modify, cancel, or liquidate orders;
  • observe orders, positions, contracts, market data, alerts, chat, or risk settings;
  • open a WebSocket or persistent broker connection;
  • create trading, production-application, paid, or financial effects; or
  • treat authenticated account metadata as authority to trade.

Demo-only controls

The reviewed client is bound to https://demo.tradovateapi.com. After OAuth it may call only GET /v1/auth/me followed by GET /v1/account/list. The intended active Demo account must be uniquely identified by its expected account ID and name under the explicit DEMO environment binding. A missing, inactive, ambiguous, or unexpected account causes a fail-closed stop.

Credentials and termination

OAuth credentials are temporary, confidential, and single-purpose. They must be isolated from source, prompts, logs, and evidence, then revoked and removed immediately after the permitted evidence is captured. Failure to verify cleanup or revocation ends the activation unsuccessfully.

No advice or warranty

The activation provides technical evidence only. It does not provide investment advice, a profitability claim, a recommendation to trade, or authorization for production. The work is experimental and may stop safely when a provider, security, or governance condition is not satisfied.

Governing project controls

The ZQOS project charter, security controls, reviewed activation package, and Project Owner dispositions remain controlling. Any broader broker capability requires a separate reviewed and expressly authorized successor horizon.
